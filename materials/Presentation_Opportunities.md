## Regeneron Science Talent Search: eligibility check first

The 2027 application deadline is **November 5, 2026, 8 p.m. Eastern**. Only individual research is eligible. The official FAQ excludes research performed as a team with another high-school student, even when students performed different parts. Research alongside adult researchers can be eligible, with clear disclosure of individual work. Because this project has two authors with substantial scientific roles, do not assume eligibility or describe it as individual work without confirming the actual contributor relationship and the rules. This is an eligibility question, not a recommendation to submit this project as-is.
